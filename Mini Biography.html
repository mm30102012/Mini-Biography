Early Life

Dr APJ Abdul Kalam was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. He came from a modest family and developed a strong interest in science and mathematics during his childhood. Despite havingunlimited limited resources, he worked hard in his studies and later studied aerospace engineering. His early life taught him the importance of discipline, education and perseverance.

Important Achievement

Dr. Kalam became one of India's most important scientists and played a major role in the country's space and missile programmes. He worked with ISRO and DRDO and contributed to the development of India's missile technology. Because of this, he became popularly known as theMissile Man of India. He later became the 11th President of India.He served the nation from 2002 to 2007.
